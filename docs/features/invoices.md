# Invoice flows

1. Open **Invoices** to search and page through payment invoices by invoice number, student name, phone, or register ID. Use the payment-date filter for a specific day.
2. Open an invoice row to view the invoice and use **Download PDF** to download a copy.
3. GST settings supply the seller values used in the print view. Each student's later instalments keep the GST treatment (inclusive or exclusive) of their first invoice, so changing a course's GST setting does not split one student's invoices.
4. **Cancel this invoice** (admins, with a reason) keeps the invoice and its number on record, stamps it CANCELLED, stops it counting toward the student's paid amount, revenue and reports, and revokes its QR verification. It cannot be undone; record a new payment if needed.

Invoice numbers are `TAI/<year>/INVnnnnnn`, with the year taken from the India date. Payment dates cannot be in the future. Formal GST credit notes and receipt resend are not implemented.
